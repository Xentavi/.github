## What we do

**Growth, as an extension of your team.** Strategy and execution across social and community, UGC, email and CRM, automation, analytics and tracking, paid media (Meta, Google, LinkedIn), brand and content, SEO and GEO. We pick the mix per business instead of selling a fixed menu.

**Software from practice.** We build the tools our own team needs, then make them products.

## Products

### Staimp &nbsp;<sub>live alpha</sub>

AI content provenance and compliance for teams that publish images under the EU AI Act.

- **Detects AI-generated and edited images** by combining several independent signals into one verdict.
- **Verifies provenance** from C2PA manifests and EXIF metadata, and flags broken or stripped records.
- **Maps each result to EU AI Act Article 50** transparency obligations with a versioned rules engine, and exports a PDF report.
- **Developer API** with API keys, OpenAPI docs and signed `scan.completed` webhooks.
- **EU-hosted** in Frankfurt. Uploads are processed in memory and never written to disk.

→ [staimp.com](https://staimp.com)

### SeeRM &nbsp;<sub>in private development</sub>

A messenger-native CRM for community-led brands on Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp and Slack.
